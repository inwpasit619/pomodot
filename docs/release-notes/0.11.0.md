## What's new

- **The card closes into the corner you dragged it to.** Drag the open card near an edge or a corner of the screen, and when it closes, the capsule tucks into that edge or corner instead of staying where the middle of the card was. Anywhere else, it closes into its middle as before. Click the capsule again and the card opens right where you left it.

## Fixed

- On a second display that sits next to another one, the capsule and the card no longer jump back when you drag them to the edge between the two.
- On a second display, the capsule now goes all the way up to that display's menu bar, and clicking anywhere on it opens the card.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

## Install or update

Download **Pomodot-0.11.0.zip** below. If you have Pomodot already, quit it first, then drag the new **Pomodot** into Applications to replace the old one. macOS asks you to confirm it once more in System Settings › Privacy & Security › **Open Anyway**, as it did the first time.

If you turned on **Open at login**, open the new Pomodot yourself once, and do Open Anyway, before you restart or log out.

Step-by-step instructions, in English and Thai, are in the [README](https://github.com/inwpasit619/pomodot#readme).

## Your data is kept

Your History and Settings are stored apart from the app, so they're all still there after you drag 0.11.0 over 0.10.0. Deleting the app keeps them too. The README explains how to remove everything, including the Open at login item.
