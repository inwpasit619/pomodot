## What's new

You can now do everything from the capsule, without touching the menu bar.

- **A menu on the card.** Click the **…** button at the top right of the card, or right-click (or Control-click) the capsule or the card, for New Cycle, Hide Floating Window, Always on Top, Statistics, Settings, About, Check for Updates and Quit. The app you're typing in keeps the keyboard.
- **The capsule always comes back.** Whenever Pomodot opens, the capsule shows, even if you hid it before quitting. The first time you hide it, Pomodot tells you how to bring it back.
- **Pomodot in the Dock.** Pomodot now has an icon in the Dock and in Command-Tab. Click it to bring the capsule back, or right-click it for the card's menu. If you'd rather not have it, switch off **Settings › General › Show in Dock**.
- **Open at login.** Switch on **Settings › General › Open at login** and Pomodot opens by itself when you log in.
- **Smaller things.** The card's buttons show their names when you rest the pointer on them, and Settings opens without picking a field for you.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

## Install or update

Download **Pomodot-0.10.0.zip** below. If you have Pomodot already, quit it first, then drag the new **Pomodot** into Applications to replace the old one. macOS asks you to confirm it once more in System Settings › Privacy & Security › **Open Anyway**, as it did the first time.

After updating, you'll see Pomodot's icon in the Dock. To hide it, switch off **Settings › General › Show in Dock**.

If you turn on **Open at login**, then on later updates open the new Pomodot yourself once, and do Open Anyway, before you restart or log out.

Step-by-step instructions, in English and Thai, are in the [README](https://github.com/inwpasit619/pomodot#readme).

## Your data is kept

Your History and Settings are stored apart from the app, so they're all still there after you drag 0.10.0 over 0.9.0. Deleting the app keeps them too. The README explains how to remove everything, including the Open at login item.
